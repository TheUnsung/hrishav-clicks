Place your Distancia font file (Distancia.woff2, Distancia.woff, Distancia.otf, or Distancia.ttf) in this folder.
The website's @font-face rule in globals.css automatically checks:
1. Locally installed 'Distancia' font on your operating system.
2. Webfont files placed here at /fonts/Distancia.*
3. Open-source extended geometric sans-serif fallback (Syncopate & Krona One).
